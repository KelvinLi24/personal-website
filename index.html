Immersive Virtual Tourism with 3DGS and AI Digital Humans

The project won China Collegiate Computing Contest Mobile Application Innovation Contest (MAIC) Hong Kong Regional Final Champion, National Final Second Prize, and was accepted as a short paper at APSCE TBICS on AI, MetaACES 2026.
