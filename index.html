Immersive Virtual Tourism with 3DGS and AI Digital Humans

Final Year Project · Grade A

Led the development of MemoSpace, an end-to-end immersive memory preservation platform integrating 3D Gaussian Splatting, XR/WebXR, and AI-driven digital humans. Independently built the MemoLens iOS AR-guided capture app using Swift and ARKit, developed MemoPortal, a Three.js/WebGL-based web platform for 3D point-cloud viewing, and contributed to the Apple Vision Pro immersive XR experience.

Managed system architecture, module integration, 3D scene data collection, and user evaluation.

The project won China Collegiate Computing Contest Mobile Application Innovation Contest (MAIC) Hong Kong Regional Final Champion, National Final Second Prize, and was accepted as a short paper at APSCE TBICS on AI, MetaACES 2026.
